    Role Types (dashboard grouping, drag to reorder)

    Role types group jobs on the dashboard: each appears as a Role Type checkbox and as a tab above the results. They do not affect scoring. Types are checked top to bottom and the first whose keywords appear in the job title wins. A type with no keywords catches everything left over and is always moved to the bottom on save.

    Tracks (drag to reorder priority)

    A track is where you would work and what it has to pay. Every job is checked against every track from top to bottom, and the first track that accepts it wins.

    Remote roles limited to other states are rejected.

    Target companies (all tracks)

    Companies you especially want to work for. Their roles are marked “Target company” in your results, and a track’s salary allowance (under More options for this track) lowers its pay floor for them. They are not scored higher.

    Who posted it

    Every role is labeled Employer, Staffing firm or Job board, and the dashboard can filter by it. The same job posted by several companies is always shown once, keeping the employer’s own posting when it was found; the other copies are listed on it.

    These copy jobs from other sites. Applying through them rarely reaches the employer.

    Job boards and reposting sites

    Company names that are not employers. A posting under one of these names is treated as a repost.

    Staffing firms (labeled, never rejected)

    Agencies filling roles for a client. These are real jobs, often the only way in; their roles are labeled “Staffing firm”. Firms whose Apply links go through staffing software (below) are recognized without being listed here.

    🎯 Welcome to JobSift

    JobSift searches Jobright.ai and company career pages, drops roles that don’t fit, and scores the rest so you can focus on the best matches.

    Setup takes about 5 minutes, and everything can be changed later.

    Before you begin:
    1. A Chromium-based browser. Microsoft Edge (pre-installed on Windows) works, and so does Google Chrome.
    2. A Jobright.ai account with a completed profile. Jobright’s match % comes from your profile, and uploading your resume is the quickest way to fill it in.

    🏢 Who posted it

    Every role is labeled by who posted it, and the dashboard can filter by it:
    Employer: the hiring company itself.
    Staffing firm: an agency filling the role for a client. A real job, and sometimes the only way in.
    Job board: a site that copied the posting from somewhere else. Applying there rarely reaches the employer.

    The same job posted by several companies is shown once, keeping the employer’s own posting when one is found.
    Firms that apply through staffing software are recognized without being listed.
