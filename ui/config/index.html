    Companies you especially want to work for. Their roles are marked “Target company” in your results, and a track’s wiggle room (under More options for this track) lowers its pay floor for them. They are not scored higher.

    Sources

    Company boards

    Companies whose job boards on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Eightfold, Oracle, Gem, iCIMS, Rippling, or Workable are searched directly. Workday, Oracle, and iCIMS boards can’t be found from a name, so for those, and for any company not found by name, add the company’s careers page link too.

    Connections

    🎯 Welcome to JobSift

    JobSift searches Jobright.ai and company career pages, drops roles that don’t fit, and scores the rest so you can focus on the best matches.

    Setup takes about 5 minutes, and everything can be changed later.

    Before you begin:
    1. A Chromium-based browser.
    2. A Jobright.ai account with a completed profile. Jobright’s match % comes from your profile, and uploading your resume is the quickest way to fill it in.

    🎯 Your first track

    A track is work location, type, arrangement and what it has to pay. For example: Remote, $90K+ and Denver hybrid, $110K+. Every job is checked against every track. You can add more tracks later.

    🏢 Company job boards (optional)

    JobSift searches Jobright.ai for the roles you listed, wherever your tracks say you want to work. It can also search companies’ own job boards on most major hiring systems, which catches postings Jobright misses. Type a company name and press Add: JobSift looks for its public job board. For Workday, Oracle, and iCIMS, also add the careers page link.

    🤝 Your connections (optional)

    If you export your connections from LinkedIn (Settings -> Data privacy -> Download your data), JobSift can mark jobs at companies where you know someone. Searching those companies’ job boards directly can be turned on later on the Connections tab.

    Your file stays on this device, no personal data is transferred out whatsoever, and JobSift uses only each person’s name and company for the purpose of showing you matches.

    You can skip this or remove the file at any time from the Connections tab.

    🎉 You’re set up

    You set your first track, the roles you want, industries and skills, company job boards, and what to avoid.
    Worth a look in the full editor:
    • More tracks: on the Tracks tab, duplicate your track and change where it is or what it pays.
    • Each track’s bar: which grades and employment types it accepts, and whether it needs an industry match.
    • Staffing firms and reposts: roles from staffing firms are labeled, and listings copied by job boards are left out. Both can be changed on the Avoid tab.
    • Roles: on the Roles tab, choose which roles are searched for, which tracks accept each one, and the dashboard tab its jobs appear under.
    • Connections: import or remove your LinkedIn connections, and check their companies’ job boards, on the Connections tab.
